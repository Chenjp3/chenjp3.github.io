My research focuses on high-energy astrophysical transients, including gamma-ray bursts, tidal disruption events, and compact binary mergers. I combine theoretical modeling, multi-wavelength observations, and hydrodynamic simulations to investigate their physical origins and emission mechanisms.

Work 01 · The Astrophysical Journal, 2026

Simulating the Late Stages of WD–BH/NS Mergers: An Origin for Fast X-Ray Transients and GRBs with Periodic Modulations

Using hydrodynamic simulations we follow the late stages of a white dwarf merging with a black hole or a neutron star. We show that the evolving accretion flow naturally produces both fast X-ray transients and gamma-ray bursts whose light curves carry periodic modulations — a distinctive observational signature of these compact binary mergers.

ApJ · 10.3847/1538-4357/ae66e8 ADS arXiv:2510.27399
Work 02 · The Astrophysical Journal Letters, 2024

Repeated Partial Disruptions in a White Dwarf–Neutron Star or White Dwarf–Black Hole Merger Modulate the Prompt Emission of Long-duration Merger-type GRBs

We propose that a white dwarf in a close orbit around a neutron star or black hole is repeatedly and partially disrupted at each pericentre passage. These repeated partial disruptions modulate the prompt emission of long-duration merger-type GRBs, offering a unified explanation for events such as GRB 230307A, 060614, and 211211A.

ApJL · 10.3847/2041-8213/ad7737 ADS arXiv:2409.00472
Work 03 · Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society, 2024

Transient quasi-periodic oscillations in the gamma-ray light curves of bright blazars

We detect and characterise transient quasi-periodic oscillations in the gamma-ray light curves of bright blazars, using their timing signatures to constrain the jet physics and the geometry of the gamma-ray emission region.

MNRAS · 10.1093/mnras/stae416 ADS arXiv:2401.10658
