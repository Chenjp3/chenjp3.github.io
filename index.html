Research

Compact Binary Mergers

How white dwarf–neutron star / black hole binaries evolve and merge, producing fast X-ray transients and merger-type gamma-ray bursts with periodic modulations from repeated partial disruptions.

Gamma-Ray Bursts

The prompt emission and central-engine physics of long-duration GRBs, connecting their light curves to the dynamics of the progenitor system through hydrodynamic simulations.

Tidal Disruption Events & Transients

Multi-wavelength signatures of stars torn apart by black holes and related fast X-ray transients, using theory, data analysis, and simulations to uncover their physical origins.

I serve as an observation duty assistant for the Einstein Probe mission (since Oct 2025) and as an affiliated scientist of the SVOM scientific team (since Nov 2024).
